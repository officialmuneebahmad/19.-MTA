## Logo Ingestion Standards Applied
1. **White Background Uniformity**: Every brand tile container (`.brand-tile`) strictly enforces a clean, pure white background (`#FFFFFF !important`) across all day and night themes, ensuring all 17 brand logos appear uniform, premium, and distraction-free.
2. **Transparent Backgrounds**: Every raster logo is a 100% transparent PNG / WebP preserving alpha channels with smooth anti-aliased edges.
3. **Responsive Scaling**: All logos fit within standard `120x48 px` containers (`object-fit: contain`) with zero clipping or distortion.
